AI SLOP ARENA

## Short description (max 300 characters)

```
A cute top-down brawler for eight players. Pick a brawler, hide in the tall grass, smash crates for power cubes and be the last one standing as the poison gas closes in. Five arenas with live weather and a day/night cycle. Play solo against bots or online with friends.
```

## About This Game (BBCode)

```
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/battle.gif[/img]

[h2]Last one standing wins[/h2]
Eight brawlers drop into the arena. Hide in the tall grass, smash crates for power cubes that make you stronger, and keep moving: the poison gas is closing in. Only one walks out.

[h2]Five brawlers, five ways to fight[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Blaster[/b]: a five-pellet shotgun spread at short range. Super: a blast that knocks enemies back and smashes walls.
[*][b]Gunslinger[/b]: a long-range six-bullet burst. Super: a twelve-bullet volley that goes through walls.
[*][b]Bomber[/b]: lobs bombs over cover. Super: a giant barrel that levels everything around it.
[*][b]Frostbite[/b]: three ice shards that slow. Super: a frost nova that freezes everyone nearby.
[*][b]Volt[/b]: an orb whose lightning chains to two enemies. Super: a storm that calls down lightning.
[/list]

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/weather.gif[/img]

[h2]Five arenas, five weathers[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Oasis[/b]: clear skies and water pools.
[*][b]Dune Storm[/b]: a sandstorm sweeps the dunes.
[*][b]Rainy Grove[/b]: rain, thunder and lightning.
[*][b]Frost Peak[/b]: snowfall and slippery ice.
[*][b]Misty Marsh[/b]: fog that shrinks your vision to a few meters.
[/list]
Every match also rolls a time of day: morning, noon, sunset or night. After dusk, lanterns light up and a head-lamp follows you, throwing long shadows ahead.

[h2]Play with friends[/h2]
Create a lobby and invite your Steam friends, or let them join straight from the friends list. Matches run peer-to-peer through Steam: no server, no port forwarding. Empty slots are filled with bots, and you can always play solo against bots.

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/daynight.gif[/img]

[h2]Made by an AI in two evenings[/h2]
The name is honest. AI SLOP ARENA was built in two evenings by Claude Opus 5.5, an AI, running on its own in Claude Code: it wrote the code, generated the art, played the game to test it and fixed what was broken. The illustrations, textures, music and sound effects are AI-generated, and the 3D figurines were modeled on a home PC from those illustrations. The human behind it wrote 35 prompts and said "that's ugly" when it was ugly. Total asset bill: $2.61.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]8-player last-one-standing matches, solo against bots or online.
[*]5 brawlers, each with a main attack and a super.
[*]5 arenas with weather that changes how you play: ice, fog, sandstorm, rain.
[*]Real-time lighting and shadows, day/night cycle, bloom and ambient occlusion.
[*]10 Steam achievements.
[*]Mouse and keyboard or gamepad, rebindable keys.
[*]Graphics presets from Low to Ultra: runs on modest PCs.
[/list]
```
