**A rank to climb, and matches that start fair.** Matchmaking now has a visible rank, every online match waits for everybody before a synchronised countdown, nobody gets jumped at spawn, and the bots follow your level. Plus a proper phone layout and much lighter rendering on mobile.

> ⚠️ **Update required to play online.** The match start changed (loading screen and countdown): versions 0.5.0 and older are asked to update. The website updates by itself.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🏅 Ranked matchmaking

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.6.0-rank.png" alt="Rank tiers from Bronze to Legend" width="100%"></p>

- A **visible rank**: RP (rank points) and tiers from 🥉 Bronze to 👑 Legend, shown in the online menu, the queue and after every ranked match (RP won or lost, new tier).
- Only matchmaking matches with **at least 2 humans** move your RP: a match alone with bots is practice, so nobody can farm bots.
- A **hidden MMR** (skill estimate) kept by the server groups players of similar level and tunes the bots of each match.

### ⏳ A fair start for everyone

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.6.0-fair.png" alt="Loading screen, countdown, spawn shield, calm bots" width="100%"></p>

- **Loading screen** like the big MOBAs: the 8 participants with their brawler, platform and loading bar. Nobody plays before everyone is in (25 s at most); shaders are compiled there, so no hitch in the first seconds.
- **3-2-1-FIGHT!** on every screen at once; the server starts the match at that moment.
- **Spawn shield**: nobody can hurt anybody during the first 5 seconds, and bots loot for 7 to 11 seconds before hunting.
- Still loading when the match starts? A bot plays your brawler until you are in, then it is yours again.

### 🤖 Bots at your level
- The bots' difficulty is never a setting: each match mixes bots around your level (your hidden MMR in matchmaking, a hidden level on your device otherwise).
- Skill now really matters: reaction time, aim, anticipation, fire rate, dodging and sight range. Sharp bots beat clumsy ones about 3 times out of 4.

### 🐞 Report a bug
- From the pause menu, the result screen, **F8**, or the prompt that appears when the game hits an error: a few words, a screenshot and the technical details, sent in one tap.

### 📱 Phones
- Every screen fits a phone held sideways: two-column menu and lobby, compact options, one-row loading screen.
- Much lighter rendering: 60 FPS cap on 90/120 Hz screens, a lower pixel ratio, mobile-specific quality presets, half the dynamic lights. Flagship phones start on Medium and climb to High when the frame rate holds.
- Android: the back button pauses the match instead of quitting the app.
