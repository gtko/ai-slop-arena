**Your brawler has a trick up its sleeve now.** Ten gadgets and ten star powers turn five brawlers into twenty playstyles, mastery unlocks them, gold crates fall from the sky, the cube leader wears a crown, bots got smarter, and a training dojo lets you try it all.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.12.0-gadgets.png" alt="The ten gadgets: mobility (Root Charge, Tail Roll, Lava Hop, Ice Slide, Blink) and utility (Bark Skin, Star Flare, Fuse Cut, Ice Wall, Overclock)" width="100%"></p>

### 🧰 Ten gadgets
- Every brawler picks one of two gadgets before the match: **mobility** (Root Charge, Tail Roll, Lava Hop, Ice Slide, Blink) or **utility** (Bark Skin, Star Flare, Fuse Cut, Ice Wall, Overclock).
- 3 charges per match and 5 seconds between two uses. Press **E** (rebindable), **LB** on a gamepad, or the small button on a touch screen.
- Using a gadget gives you away if you hide in a bush, like firing does.

### ⭐ Ten star powers and mastery
- One passive of two per brawler: faster healing, splintering seeds, steadier aim, burning puddles, deeper freezes, an extra lightning jump...
- Every match with a brawler earns mastery: level 2 unlocks its second gadget, level 4 its second star power, with a little ceremony on the result screen.
- Pick your loadout under the brawler cards.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.12.0-more.png" alt="Six cards: star powers, mastery, supply drops, bounty crown, smarter bots, training dojo" width="100%"></p>

### 🪂 A livelier arena
- **Supply drops**: twice per match a beacon and a siren announce a gold crate falling from the sky, with 3 power cubes inside.
- **Bounty crown**: whoever carries the most cubes (5 or more) wears a crown; knock them out for 2 extra cubes. It only shows when you can see its wearer. Cubes now give +300 health (was +400), and brawlers grow a little with them.
- **Supers you can see coming**: Frostbite's nova grows on the ground before it freezes, the meteor casts a shadow where it will land, and your own super slashes your portrait across the screen.

### 🤖 Smarter bots
- They back off to cover to heal, sidestep your shots, walk out of hazards, use their gadgets and save their supers for the right moment.
- Balance check on 100 bot matches: every brawler now finishes top 4 in 44-58% of its matches.

### 🥋 Training dojo and stats
- **TRAINING** next to Solo: dummies, no gas, unlimited gadgets and a damage-per-second meter.
- The result screen shows your damage, KOs, cubes and gadgets, and the MVP of the match.
- Phones draw lighter figurines on lower quality settings (up to 3x fewer triangles).
