**Something bites in the bushes, and the islands are falling.** A sky map where one push sends you into the void, arenas you can play with (jump pads, barrels, bridges, mushrooms), and two new brawlers: Pip & Chomp, the bush hunter, and Mochi, the sumo seal.

> ⚠️ Update required to play online: 0.14 apps are asked to update.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.15.0-isles.png" alt="Windmill Isles and the map kit: ring-outs, bridges, jump pads, barrels, mushrooms, arenas that react" width="100%"></p>

### 🏝️ New map: Windmill Isles
- Floating islands around a windmill, joined by wooden bridges and jump pads, over the void.
- **Ring-outs**: pushed into the void, you fall. The K.O. goes to whoever pushed you.
- **No gas**: the outer islands crumble at 40 s and 60 s (5 s of cracks first), the bridges at 80 s, then the middle island shrinks; the gas only comes at the very end.
- Its own theme (accordion and taiko).

### 💥 Arenas you can play with
- **Jump pads** throw you to the next ground toward the middle, and the landing hits enemies around. Also on Oasis.
- **Explosive barrels**: 900 in 2.5 m, a big push, and the next barrel goes too. Also on Dunes.
- **Bridges** break under explosions, **healing mushrooms** give 1,200 health and grow back.
- **Arenas react**: footprints and dust in sand, snow and mud, rubble where walls broke, a light on the cube of a broken crate.

### 🌿🍡 Two new brawlers

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.15.0-brawlers.png" alt="Pip & Chomp and Mochi" width="100%"></p>

- **Pip & Chomp** (assassin): Chomp lunges 4 m and bites (it bends toward an enemy you can see), Pip says sorry. Super **Venus Trap**: a bush that bites, roots and reveals the first enemy who walks in. Gadgets Leap Snack and Spore Puff.
- **Mochi** (tank, 6,800 HP): a belly bump of 3 jelly waves that push back, ideally off a cliff. Super **Mochi Pound**: a 9 m leap that crushes, stuns and flattens walls. Gadgets Belly Slide and Sticky Mochi.
- Each unlocks for 1,500 Slop Coins or 240 Gems; try them free in Training.

### 🧹 Also
- **You can tell when you are hidden**: in a bush your brawler turns see-through, a closed eye sits over your name and the screen edges turn green; shoot or get hit and the eye opens with a countdown. (Nothing tells you an enemy is close enough to spot you: that would give them away.)
- Nurse Kappa's beak is yellow again.
- Balance measured over 200 bot matches: every brawler within 39-60% top 4.
