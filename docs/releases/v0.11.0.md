**Every hit lands, and you feel it.** Hits freeze and squash, the camera breathes with the fight, KOs get a stamp and a slow-motion finale, 24 new sounds, a big health bar, clearer colours, and online matches start within a minute.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.10.x still plays online with 0.11.0. Online, the matchmaking change and the new immunity rule apply to everyone at once (the server runs the matches).

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.11.0-impact.png" alt="Six cards: hit freeze, living camera, KO beat, big health bar, readable chaos, fairer fights" width="100%"></p>

### 💥 Every hit lands
- Each weapon freezes the victim's pose for a split second (20 to 120 ms), then the figurine squashes and springs back like jelly, with a white flash.
- Pellets and bursts of one attack add up into a single number that counts up.
- Landing hits in a row plays a hit sound that rises in pitch; your phone (Android) or gamepad buzzes a little.
- Hit sparks fly on in the direction of the shot.

### 🎥 A living camera
- A smooth shake with a little roll instead of a random jitter, and a small kick when you fire.
- It looks ahead toward where you aim, zooms out for the last three and the final duel, and in when you lurk in a bush.

### 💀 KOs you remember
- Bodies hold, then fly off away from the killer; a K.O. stamp marks your victims.
- A kill feed at the top right, and 3 LEFT and FINAL DUEL banners with their own musical stings.
- Offline, the final KO plays in slow motion while the camera circles the winner.

### ❤️ Your health, where you look
- A big health bar at the bottom centre, with your ammo, replaces the key hints (the controls stay in Options).
- Under 30% health the screen edges beat red with a heartbeat and the music goes muffled; a bar that is healing glows.

### 👀 Readable chaos
- A bright blue outline for you, dark red for enemies; **Options > General > Colour-blind colours** turns it into blue against orange.
- Bigger weapons, and eyes that react: happy after a KO, squinting when hit, glaring when attacking, tired at low health.
- The next gas ring blinks on the floor for the last 6 seconds.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.11.0-sound.png" alt="Four cards: own weapon sounds, brawler voices, footsteps, match stings" width="100%"></p>

### 🔊 24 new sounds
- Gunslinger, Frostbite and Volt get their own shots; every brawler shouts on its super and cheers after a KO.
- Footsteps on sand, grass, snow and mud.
- Hide and seek stays intact: what you cannot see, you cannot hear either.

### ⚖️ Fairer fights
- After a freeze, 1.5 s of immunity: no more frozen chains.
- Online, bots fill the match after 1 minute instead of 5, and lonely players are grouped together first.
- Bots in their calm opening seconds no longer clip players with a crate shot.
