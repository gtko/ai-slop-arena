**No more fiddling with settings.** The game now picks its own graphics quality from your graphics card, then keeps adjusting it to the frame rate while you play. And the options get a new **General** tab.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.4.0-auto.png" alt="How automatic quality works" width="100%"></p>

## ✨ Highlights

### 🧠 Automatic graphics quality
- **Auto** is the new default preset.
- **First launch**: the game reads your graphics card and picks a starting tier: *Ultra* for RTX, Radeon RX 5000+ or Apple M2+, *High* for other dedicated cards, *Medium* for integrated graphics, *Low* for phones.
- **While you play**: the real frame rate is measured every 4 seconds. Under 45 FPS, one step down; below *Low*, the render resolution drops to 60% then 50%. After about 20 smooth seconds, one step back up, never above the detected tier.
- The result is **remembered** between sessions. Pick any preset by hand to switch Auto off; **Detect** in the Graphics tab runs it again.

### ⚙️ New General tab

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.4.0-general.png" alt="The new General tab and FPS counter" width="100%"></p>

- **Language**, **fullscreen**, **FPS counter**, **camera shake** and **graphics quality**, all in one place, and it opens first.
- The FPS counter shows the tier Auto picked, e.g. *60 FPS · Auto Ultra*.
- The Graphics tab shows your detected graphics card.
- Players still on the old untouched default (*High*) move to Auto.
