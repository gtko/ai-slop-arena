**The arena gets a real soundtrack.** Ten new full-length songs, and a playlist for every moment of the game, so a long session no longer loops the same 30 seconds.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.3.0-tracklist.png" alt="Tracklist: 10 songs with their waveforms" width="100%"></p>

## ✨ Highlights

### 🎵 10 new songs
- The **five map themes** are now full songs of 2 to 3 minutes (they were 30-second loops): Oasis, Dune Storm, Rainy Grove, Frost Peak, Misty Marsh.
- **Five new themes**: a second battle theme, the **final showdown**, a second lobby theme, the **online room** and a **night** theme.
- Made with Google Lyria 3 Pro, instrumental. All ten cost **$0.88**.

### 🔁 A playlist for every moment

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.3.0-playlists.png" alt="Which songs play when" width="80%"></p>

- When a song ends, the next one takes over.
- ⚔️ **Final showdown**: when only 3 brawlers are left, the music switches to the final theme until the result.
- 🌙 At night, a match opens with the night theme.

### 🔉 Quieter start
- The master volume now starts at **50%** (it was far too loud on the first notes). Existing mixes are brought down to 50% once.

### ⚙️ Under the hood
- Music now **streams** instead of being decoded up front: twelve full songs decoded would have taken about 600 MB of RAM, a problem on phones.
- The desktop app serves byte ranges, so streamed music can seek.
