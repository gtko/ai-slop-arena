**A hotfix: every human now hits at full strength, wherever the match runs.** Plus the victory fanfare stops when you play again, and a new roadmap page shows what comes next.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.10.0 still plays online with 0.10.1. Online damage is computed by the server, so the fix applies to every online match right away. In a Steam lobby, the host needs 0.10.1.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.10.1-damage.png" alt="Damage before and after: Steam lobby friends and online players went from 85% to 100%, solo and the Steam host stay at 100%, bots stay at 85%" width="100%"></p>

### ⚖️ Same damage for every human
- Online, every player dealt 85% damage instead of 100%: the full-damage bonus went to the player of the machine running the match, and the server has none. Online players now hit as hard as in solo, so fights between humans are shorter.
- In a Steam lobby, the host dealt 100% and their friends 85%. Everyone now deals 100%.
- A player who leaves mid-match becomes a bot (85%) and gets 100% back when they rejoin, power cubes included.
- Bots keep 85%: they aim with lead prediction.

### 🎺 The fanfare knows when to stop
- After a win, the 30-second victory fanfare kept playing over the next match, the menu or the lobby. It now fades out as soon as the next song starts.

### 🗺️ A roadmap page
- [ai-slop-arena.gtux-prog.workers.dev/roadmap](https://ai-slop-arena.gtux-prog.workers.dev/roadmap) (in French): six upcoming versions, seven new brawlers with their concept art, new arenas, and the ideas beyond. It came out of a five-round brainstorm between two AI game designers, detailed in [docs/roadmap.md](https://github.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md).
