**AI SLOP ARENA leaves the browser.** The same brawler now ships as a desktop app with full Steam integration, as an Epic Games Store build, and as Android and iOS apps with touch controls. Every version can play with every other one.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.2.0-platforms.png" alt="Web, Steam, Epic, Android and iOS" width="100%"></p>

## ✨ Highlights

### 🎮 Steam
- 🏆 **10 achievements**, from *First Blood* to *Centurion* (100 KOs), plus stats. Progress earned offline unlocks the next time Steam runs.
- 👥 **Friends**: invite from the overlay, or join a friend in one click from your friends list, even with the game closed. Rich presence shows what you are doing.
- 📡 **Steam networking**: rooms are Steam lobbies, the lobby owner hosts over Steam P2P (no port forwarding, no server). If the host leaves, someone else takes over.
- ⌨️ Overlay (Shift+Tab), fullscreen with F11 or Alt+Enter, a Quit button.

### 🔀 Cross-play
- Steam players can open a **cross-play room** on the web relay: browser, Epic and mobile players join it with its code.
- Joining by code looks for a Steam lobby first, then a web room, so Steam players can also enter any browser room.

### 📱 Android and iOS

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.2.0-touch.png" alt="Touch controls" width="100%"></p>

- **Touch controls**, Brawl Stars style: floating move stick, attack stick (tap: nearest enemy, drag: aim, release: fire), super button with a charge ring, pause button.
- Landscape only, full screen, notch-safe HUD. Phones start on the Low graphics preset.

### 🛒 Epic Games Store
- A desktop build without Steam: the Epic launcher gives your display name and language, online play goes through the web rooms.

### 🌍 30 languages
- Every language Steam supports, from French to Japanese and Arabic (right-to-left). The game follows Steam's language, then your system's; **Options → Language** overrides it.

### 🕸️ Website
- Steam buttons with the Steam logo and an *Also on Steam* section on [the website](https://ai-slop-arena.gtux-prog.workers.dev/).
