**Five new achievements, and Google Play Games on Android.** There are now 15 achievements to unlock, on Steam and on Google Play Games, and the Android app shows them in Play's own achievements screen.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.6.0 and 0.6.1 still play online with 0.6.2.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🏆 Five new achievements

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.6.2-achievements.png" alt="The five new achievements: Podium Finish, Super Finish, Night Owl, Crate Crusher, Champion" width="100%"></p>

- **Podium Finish**: finish a match in the top 3.
- **Super Finish**: knock out a brawler with your super.
- **Night Owl**: win a match at night (press T, or let the day/night cycle run).
- **Crate Crusher**: break 50 crates.
- **Champion**: win 10 matches.
- They count online too, and what you already did still counts: your earlier wins count toward Champion. Progress earned offline is sent the next time Steam or Play Games is there.
- Names and descriptions in all 30 languages.

### 🤖 Google Play Games (Android)
- The Android app signs in to Play Games automatically and unlocks the same 15 achievements there, with progress bars for Veteran, Centurion, Crate Crusher and Champion.
- A new **ACHIEVEMENTS** button in the menu opens Play's achievements screen (it appears once the game's Play Games project is live).
