**The arenas come alive.** Fourteen animated animals roam the six maps, Windmill Isles is rebuilt around its windmill, and ponds, frozen lakes and island coasts lose their square corners.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.16.0-fauna.png" alt="The 14 animals: fennec, arctic fox, cat, hedgehog, squirrel, frog, hare, penguin, duck, hen, sparrow, lizard, vulture, raven" width="100%"></p>

### 🦊 Living arenas
- **14 figurine animals**, rigged and animated like the brawlers: fennecs, lizards and vultures on Oasis; fennecs, scorpions, tumbleweeds and dust devils in the Dune Storm; frogs, hedgehogs, squirrels and ducks in the Rainy Grove; penguins sliding on their bellies, snow hares, arctic foxes and ravens on Frost Peak; fireflies, frogs and ducks in the Misty Marsh; hens, a cat, sparrows and bees on Windmill Isles.
- They walk, hop, peck, sniff, sit, groom, fly and glide, and run away when a brawler comes close.
- **They never give you away**: animals only react to brawlers you can already see, so nothing flees from the bush you hide in.
- Butterflies, bees, fireflies, dragonflies, seeds and dust fill the air. The amount follows your graphics quality.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.16.0-isles.png" alt="Windmill Isles rebuilt: pinwheel islands, the windmill in the middle, islands that shake then drop, the sky below, rounded decor" width="100%"></p>

### 🏝️ Windmill Isles, rebuilt
- A pinwheel of **round islands** (4 big ones, 4 islets) around a round middle island, with a **bigger windmill right in the centre**.
- **Nobody spawns on the middle island** any more.
- A doomed island **cracks, shakes and sheds pebbles** for 5 s, then drops away in one piece (no more blinking squares).
- Crumble order: islets at 40 s, big islands at 60 s, bridges at 80 s, then the middle shrinks down to a ring around the windmill.
- An **open sky below**: clouds at every depth, gulls, far islets with tiny windmills.

### 🌊 Rounder, more natural decor
- **Ponds** get rounded shores with sandy or muddy banks, pebbles and reeds; **ice** becomes frozen lakes ringed with rocks; island coasts are soft with earth and rock walls underneath. Collisions still follow the tiles.

### 🧹 Also
- Jump pads are back to Windmill Isles only (they left Oasis).
- A dark frame around the sky on Windmill Isles is gone.
