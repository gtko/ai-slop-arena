**Blaster, Gunslinger and Bomber are brand-new characters.** Same names, same moves, same numbers: only the looks changed, from the 3D figurines to the projectiles, the portraits and every text.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.7.x still plays online with 0.8.0.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.8.0-brawlers.png" alt="The three new characters: a tree-stump golem, an axolotl star-ranger and a magma imp" width="100%"></p>

### 🌳 Blaster, the tree-stump golem
- A stocky golem of bark and moss with a crown of leaves, firing a hollow-log blunderbuss.
- His shots are spinning thorny seeds: 5 per shot, 9 with the super, which still knocks enemies back and smashes walls.

### 🦎 Gunslinger, the axolotl star-ranger
- A pink axolotl in a retro space-ranger suit, with a twin ray pistol in each hand.
- Bursts of 6 lavender ray bolts; the super fires 12 that go straight through walls.

### 🔥 Bomber, the magma imp
- A little imp of volcanic rock and glowing lava, with a crown of flames that flickers as he moves.
- He lobs fireballs with a flame trail over walls; his super calls down a meteor that crashes on the same spot, at the same moment, for 1800 damage.

### 🎨 Everywhere
- New menu portraits, colours, app icon and splash screen, store graphics, website trailer and screenshots.
- New descriptions in all 30 languages.
