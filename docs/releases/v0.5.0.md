**Online, for real.** One matchmaking queue for every platform, matches played on our server (no more host advantage, no more wallhacks) and moderation that a few sore losers cannot abuse.

> ⚠️ **Update required to play online.** Online play now speaks a new protocol: versions 0.4.0 and older are asked to update. The website updates by itself; download the new desktop and mobile builds below.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🔎 Find a match

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.5.0-matchmaking.png" alt="The matchmaking queue" width="100%"></p>

- **One queue for web, Steam, Epic, Android and iOS**: press *Find a match* in the online menu.
- The queue screen shows the players waiting, their platforms, your waiting time and when bots come in.
- **8 players**: the match starts right away. **After 5 minutes**, whoever is queued plays and **bots fill the empty slots**. **Play now with bots** skips the wait.
- Room codes and Steam lobbies are still there to play with friends.

### 🛡️ The server runs the match

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.5.0-server.png" alt="How the server protects each match" width="100%"></p>

- Matchmaking and room-code matches are played **on our server** with the game's own rules; players only send their inputs. Nobody hosts, so nobody can cheat as the host.
- **Every move is checked** (speed, walls, frozen, knockback); impossible moves are refused and repeated ones get the player kicked.
- **No more wallhacks**: each player only receives the brawlers they can see. Bushes and fog are enforced by the server.
- Steam friend lobbies stay peer-to-peer, with the same checks on the host.

### ⚖️ Moderation
- ⚑ **Report** a player (cheating, offensive name, bad behaviour) and ✕ **remove** someone from your room.
- **Fair automatic bans**: a player is banned only if reported in at least **30% of 10+ matches**, by **4+ different players** (24 h, then 7 days on a repeat). Proven cheating detected by the server is banned for 7 days.
- Players have no account: bans use a random id kept on the device, never personal data.

### 🧪 Under the hood
- The game rules now also build as a headless server (`vite build --mode server`), tested by `npm test`.
- 20 new strings in all 30 languages.
