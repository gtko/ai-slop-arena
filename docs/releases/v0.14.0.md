**Bring a friend. Bring them back.** Duo Showdown puts you in a team of two against three other pairs: revive your knocked-out partner, ping where to go, queue together with a friend. And a new brawler who keeps you both alive: Nurse Kappa.

> ⚠️ Update required to play online: 0.13 apps are asked to update.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.14.0-duo.png" alt="Six cards: Duo Showdown, Buddy Revive, 2 hearts a team, queue together, pings, bot partners" width="100%"></p>

### 🤝 Duo Showdown
- **4 teams of 2** on the five arenas. Partners start side by side, their shots fly through each other, and the last team standing wins. The gas waits a little longer (35 s) so you can regroup.
- **Buddy Revive**: a knocked-out partner leaves a ghost for 15 s. Stand next to it for 3 s and they are back at 40% health. Being hit pauses the revive, the gas ends the ghost, and each team has **2 revives** a match.
- **You see what your partner sees**, bushes included. Nothing ever shows an enemy neither of you can see.
- Pick **Showdown** or **Duo** on the home screen: solo against bots (with a bot partner), or in the online queue. Room leaders pick the mode for their room.

### 👯 Better with friends
- **Queue together**: a room of friends goes into the matchmaking queue as one ticket (2 for Duo, up to 4 for Showdown). In Duo you are one team.
- **Pings** (G, the X button, or 📍 on phones): go, attack, loot or danger, depending on what you aim at. Only your partner sees them.
- **Team HUD**: your partner's health and revive hearts, an arrow when they are off screen, and a line on the ground between you.
- **Bot partners** follow you, run to revive you and focus your target. Bots now say thanks when you bring them back.
- **Ranked Duo**: RP per team placement (+30, +12, −4, −10). Two friends alone with bots do not move RP.

### 🐢 New brawler: Nurse Kappa

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.14.0-kappa.png" alt="Nurse Kappa: soap bubble, Tidal Wave, River Dive, Bowl Splash, star powers" width="100%"></p>

- A bossy river-imp nurse: **soap bubbles** lobbed over walls (700 splash) heal her when they hit, and heal her Duo partner caught in the splash.
- **Super, Tidal Wave**: a 10 m wave sweeps enemies away and parts the gas for a moment.
- Gadgets **River Dive** and **Bowl Splash**, star powers **Hydrotherapy** and **Undertow**.
- Unlock her with 1,500 Slop Coins or 240 Gems, or try her for free in Training.
