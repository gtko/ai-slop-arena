**Played with the kids, fixed for the kids.** A shorter wait online, skins you can earn by playing, a game five times lighter on tablets, a home screen that fits every window, and mobile apps that now update themselves.

> 📱 **Android and iOS players: install this version once.** From now on the app downloads new versions by itself (on Wi-Fi) and applies them at the next launch.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.16.1-changes.png" alt="Half the wait online, skins for Slop Coins, five times lighter on tablets, lighter on PCs, a home screen that fits, the app updates itself" width="100%"></p>

### ⏱️ Half the wait online
- Matchmaking fills the match with bots after **30 s** instead of 1 minute, and still looks for players of your level first.

### 🪙 Skins for Slop Coins
- Every shop skin can now be bought with **1,000 Slop Coins** (about 50 matches) or 290 Gems. The golden skin is still earned at mastery 10.

### 📱 Lighter on tablets and phones
- Lighter trees, bushes and brawlers on mobile, fewer trees out of view, no shadows from small things: up to **5× fewer triangles** a frame (Rainy Grove on mobile low: 3.56 M → 0.71 M).
- On PCs, shadows are drawn once a frame instead of twice: about 30% fewer triangles, same picture.

### 🧩 A home screen that fits
- The 8 brawler cards keep their width and show their full names.
- On touch screens gadgets and star powers are one row of icons and the skins start folded; on any window the hero column shrinks so it never runs under the skins and the roster.

### 🔄 The app updates itself
- Android and iOS apps check for a new version on the home screen, download it on Wi-Fi only, and switch at the next launch (never during a match). A version that fails to start is rolled back automatically.
