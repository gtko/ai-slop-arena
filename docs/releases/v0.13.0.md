**Every match earns something now, and everyone sees it.** Account levels, daily and weekly quests, a Bot League with its Trophy Road, Slop Coins and a daily shop, cosmetics you wear online, an emote wheel, a result podium, bots with personalities, an event on every arena and a new Chaos twist every week.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.13.0-progress.png" alt="Six cards: account level, quests, Bot League, Slop Coins, cosmetics, podium" width="100%"></p>

### ⭐ Levels, quests and the Bot League
- **Account level**: about 55 XP a match. Every level pays Slop Coins and 16 of them give a cosmetic, with a chest and reward cards on the result screen.
- **Quests**: 3 daily quests and a weekly one, pinned on a cork board and stamped when done. Swap one a day with 🎲.
- **Bot League**: solo matches earn trophies per brawler, and 20 rewards wait on the **Trophy Road**. Your bots get a little sharper as you climb.
- The result screen shows your XP, coins, trophies and quest progress.

### 🎨 Cosmetics, shop and collection
- 2 recolours per brawler, a **golden figurine** at mastery 10, 5 trails, 5 K.O. effects, 12 emotes, 10 frames, 14 titles and 30 profile icons.
- **Slop Coins** are earned only by playing. The shop changes every day: one featured skin (-20%) and four other items. Looks only, nothing makes you stronger.
- Everyone sees what you wear: in the arena, in the room list, on the loading screen and on the podium.

### 😀 Emotes, podium and bot personalities
- **Emote wheel**: B, the right stick click or the 😀 button on phones. An emote reveals you for a moment, even in a bush.
- **Podium**: the top 3 on steps, plus the MVP and awards for the most K.O.s, cubes, supers, gadgets, crates and emotes.
- Bots are characters now: hunter, camper, looter, vulture, coward and show-off, each with its habits, an icon and lines in speech bubbles. They answer your emotes.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.13.0-arenas.png" alt="The five arena events and the five Weekly Chaos mutators" width="100%"></p>

### 🌋 The arenas fight back
- About a minute into every match, the arena joins in: **geysers** on the Oasis, a **meteor shower** on the Dunes (it breaks walls and crates), a **lightning rod** in the Grove, a **blizzard** on Frost Peak, a **fog bank** with healing mushrooms in the Marsh.
- Every hit is telegraphed on the ground first, and bots step out of the way too.

### 🌀 Weekly Chaos
- One twist a week, the same for everyone: Cube Rain, Night Hunt, Gas Breath, Super Rush or Gadget Frenzy.
- Switch it on in solo, or for your room if you lead it. Ranked matchmaking never uses it. Win a Chaos match for the "Agent of Chaos" title.
