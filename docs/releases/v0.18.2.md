**Lighter everywhere.** The game moves to Godot 4.5, and phones and tablets get a much lighter Low quality: fewer triangles, half the video memory, simpler shaders and less work every frame. A new performance overlay shows what slows your device down.

> 📱 **Android players on Google Play: keep your app as it is** for now. The APK below is a separate test build.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.18.2-changes.png" alt="Godot 4.5.2, 2-3x fewer triangles on Low, half the video memory, lighter shaders on Low, less work every frame, a performance overlay" width="100%"></p>

### ⚡ Godot 4.5.2
- The Android, Windows and Linux builds ship their shaders already compiled: fewer hitches the first time an effect shows up.

### 📉 Much lighter on Low
- **2-3x fewer triangles**: about 120-240k a frame instead of 220-630k, with lighter copies of the trees, rocks, walls, crates and bushes, brawlers at about 5k triangles each, and only the trees on screen drawn.
- **Half the video memory**: the model textures are now compressed for the GPU (Low: 101-126 MB down to 50-59 MB; High saves about 50 MB too).
- **Lighter shaders**: simpler materials and no more full-screen copy for the hidden areas: 16-28 % less GPU time on our tests.
- **Less work every frame**: fewer animals, lights and menu effects on Low phones; quality changes during a match wait for the next one instead of freezing it.

### 📊 See what slows you down
- **Options › General › Show performance**: fps, frame time, GPU time, triangles, render size, quality, renderer and what limits your device. A screenshot of it is the best bug report for a slow device.
