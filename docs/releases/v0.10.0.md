**Walls hide you now.** Brawlers behind walls, trees and crates, or beyond your vision range, disappear from your screen, and a new lower, wider camera shows the characters' faces.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.9.x still plays online with 0.10.0.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.10.0-sight.png" alt="Top-down view of the line of sight: walls cast vision shadows, an enemy behind a wall, in a bush and too far away stays hidden" width="100%"></p>

### 👁️ Line of sight
- Walls, trees, crates and props block your view, just like they block bullets. An enemy who ducks behind cover vanishes, health bar included; one peeking around a corner still shows.
- You see 14 m around you, 11 m inside the Dune Storm sandstorm. Beyond that, enemies are hidden too.
- What you cannot see is greyed out on screen, so you always know where an ambush can come from. In the sandstorm, hidden ground drowns in dust.
- Bushes work as before: you only spot a brawler inside when you are close or when they shoot.
- Bots follow the same rules: no more aimbots through walls. They remember a target that just ducked out of sight for 2.5 s.
- Online, the server only sends each player what they can see, so there is nothing to wallhack.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.10.0-camera.png" alt="The new camera: lower angle, further back, clear weather around the player" width="100%"></p>

### 🎥 A new camera
- Lower angle (47° instead of 57°): you see the brawlers' faces and animations instead of the tops of their heads.
- Further back, with about twice as much of the arena on screen.
- Rain and sandstorm fog now start past your brawler instead of burying it.
