**A new home screen, built like a game client.** A top navigation bar, your brawler fighting live behind the menu, a skins carousel, a shop with real navigation, and two currencies: Slop Coins you earn to unlock brawlers, and Gems for brawlers and looks.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.13.0 still plays online with 0.13.1.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.13.1-home.png" alt="Six cards: top navigation, live showcase, skins carousel, shop with tabs, Slop Coins, Gems" width="100%"></p>

### 🏠 A new home screen
- **Top navigation**: Play, Collection, Shop, Quests and Trophy Road, with your Slop Coins, Gems, trophies and profile always in view. Collection, Shop, Quests and the Trophy Road open as full pages.
- **Live showcase**: your brawler fights behind the menu with your looks, on the map you picked, and the camera follows it. Pick another brawler or map and the match restarts around it.
- **Play panel**: a big ONLINE button, Solo and Training beside it, the map picker, the Weekly Chaos switch and today's quests at a glance.
- **Skins carousel**: every skin of the chosen brawler right above the brawler row, one click to wear it.

### 🛒 A shop you can browse, and two currencies
- Tabs: **Featured**, **Brawlers**, **Skins**, **Effects**, **Emotes**, **Profile** and **Gems**.
- **Slop Coins** are earned by playing (matches, quests, levels, the Trophy Road) and unlock brawlers.
- **Gems** unlock brawlers and looks. Gem packs are listed; payment opens soon on every platform.
- Blaster, Gunslinger and Bomber are free to everyone; Frostbite and Volt cost 1,500 Slop Coins (or Gems) for new players. **Everyone who played before keeps all five.** Try any brawler for free in Training.

### 🧹 Polished everywhere
- Five testers went through every screen twice on desktop, phones and 11 languages.
- Fixed: a rare freeze of the menu's background match, a GPU texture leak per match, the result screen now counts quest XP, Tab no longer opens the lighting panel, and many layout fixes on phones.
- Heavy display fonts for Cyrillic, Greek, Vietnamese, Thai, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
