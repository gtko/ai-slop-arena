**Better together.** Duo partners can revive each other again, every gadget and star power has a short preview clip, all eight brawlers are free, and phones and tablets get a much lighter game.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.18.1-changes.png" alt="Revive your Duo partner, see every power before you pick, all eight brawlers are free, smoother on phones and tablets, lighter arenas, a menu on the home screen" width="100%"></p>

### 💚 Duo: revive your partner
- A knocked-out partner leaves a ghost: stand **3 s** next to it to bring them back (hits pause the count). A tether on the ground, a partner card with their health and revive hearts, and an arrow when they're off screen show you the way.
- Your partner is green, their shots pass through you, the camera follows them while you wait, and their win is your win.

### 🎬 See every power before you pick
- **32 short clips** (16 gadgets, 16 star powers), filmed with the real game rules: hover a gadget or star power, tap its ▶, or focus it with a gamepad.
- Fixed on the way: everyone's gadget B looked like gadget A in matches, Frostbite's Ice Wall was invisible, and Splinters, Big Bang, Permafrost, Surge and Steady Aim were drawn smaller or missing.

### 🔓 All eight brawlers are free
- Every player owns the whole roster. Brawlers added later will cost Slop Coins or Gems.

### 📱 Smoother on phones and tablets
- Phones and tablets draw the whole frame within a pixel budget: a 2880x1800 tablet on Low now draws **1.3 MP instead of 5.2 MP**.
- Lighter arenas: bushes **3,571 → 1,070** triangles, brawlers with **4x fewer** vertices, up to **47 % fewer** draw calls on Low.
- The quality adapts on its own and can settle on a steady 30 fps.

### ⏸️ A menu on the home screen
- Escape (or the Back button) opens resume, options and quit.
