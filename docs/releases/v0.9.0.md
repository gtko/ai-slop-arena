**The brawlers come alive.** Every character now has a real skeleton and 16 hand-tuned animations, soft parts that move with the wind, eyes that blink and weapons held in their hands.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.8.x still plays online with 0.9.0.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.9.0-animations.png" alt="Six of the sixteen animations of each brawler: run, aim, victory, wave, hiding in a bush, knocked out" width="100%"></p>

### 🦴 Real skeletons, 16 animations each
- A standard humanoid skeleton (Mixamo bone names) in every model, with joints placed by hand on each character: the head turns as one piece, elbows and knees bend where they should.
- Idle, run, sneak and peek in bushes, slide on ice, aim, shoot, super, hit, knocked-out fall, victory dance, wave, a bored sigh, a personal fidget for each brawler, a cough in the poison gas and a fist pump after a knockout.
- Aiming and shooting only move the upper body, so brawlers keep running while they fire.
- The rigged characters also ship as `.glb`, `.fbx` and `.blend` files, ready for Blender, Unity, Unreal or Godot.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.9.0-alive.png" alt="What makes them feel alive: skeletons, wind, blinks, weapons in hand, bush sneaking, big moments" width="100%"></p>

### 🍃 Wind in their hair
- Blaster's leaves, Bomber's flames, Gunslinger's gills and tail, Frostbite's cape and hair, Volt's antennas sway in the wind (harder in storms), trail behind when running and bounce when stopping.

### 👀 Little touches
- Everyone blinks every few seconds, sometimes twice; eyes close when knocked out.
- Weapons are separate objects held in the hand; a brawler puts its weapon away at the belt to wave hello.
- Arms, fists and weapons keep clear of the huge chibi heads in every animation.
