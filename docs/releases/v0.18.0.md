**One engine for every version of the game.** Play on the website now opens the Godot game, every download is built with Godot, it runs much smoother in the browser, and your web progress comes along.

> 📱 **Android players on Google Play: keep your app as it is.** The Play Store version stays the current one for now and keeps your progress; the Godot version reaches the store once it can bring your Android progress along. The APK below is a separate test build: it cannot replace the Play app.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.18.0-changes.png" alt="Play opens the Godot game, smoother in your browser, far fewer GPU calls, your web progress comes along, a loader that never hangs, invite links still work" width="100%"></p>

### 🎮 Godot everywhere
- **Play** on the website opens the Godot game, and the Windows (Steam and Epic), Linux, Android, iOS and web downloads are all Godot builds. The old three.js version is gone.
- Same matches as before: everyone plays together, whatever version they run.
- Invite links (`/play?room=CODE`) still drop you straight into your friend's room.

### 💾 Your web progress comes along
- The first time you open the new version in the same browser, it picks up your coins, gems, brawlers, skins, trophies, settings and your rank.

### ⚡ Much smoother in the browser
- About **16,000 graphics calls a frame down to about 2,300** on the home screen: menu from 48 to about 80 fps, matches from 41 to 75 fps on our test PC.
- Quality adapts to your graphics card and to the frame rate it measures, and stays sharp on high-DPI screens without drawing four times the pixels.

### 🌱 A loader that never hangs
- While the engine starts, "Starting..." keeps moving instead of freezing on 100 %; the percentage never goes past 100 %, and if something fails you get a Reload button instead of a stuck page.
