**The Godot preview goes online.** Play the new Godot client in your browser at [/godot/](https://ai-slop-arena.gtux-prog.workers.dev/godot/), in the same matches as everyone, behind a loading screen with a little story.

## ✨ Highlights

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.17.1-changes.png" alt="Play the Godot client in your browser, served from Cloudflare R2, a loader with a story, Gunslinger shows up, same gag on PC and phones, the road to Godot everywhere" width="100%"></p>

### 🌐 The Godot preview in your browser
- The Godot client is online at **/godot/**, linked from the home page. It plays in the same matches as the web, Steam and mobile players.
- Its files come from Cloudflare R2, one folder per version: the engine shrinks from 44 MB to 9 MB on the wire and every file is cached for good.

### 🌱 A loading screen with a story
- While the game downloads, Blaster walks in and shoots the title down, **one letter for every 9 %** of the download.
- When it's done, Gunslinger skids in, two bolts later Blaster runs away through the fallen letters, and the game starts.
- The same gag plays as a 3.6 s intro with the real 3D models on PC and phones (any key or tap skips it). Reduced-motion settings get a calm version.

### 🗺️ The road to Godot everywhere
- The plan to switch every build to Godot, once nothing players have today is lost (offline solo, accounts, Steam, progress), is in [`docs/godot-migration.md`](https://github.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/blob/main/docs/godot-migration.md).
