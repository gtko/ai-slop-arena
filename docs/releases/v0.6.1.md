**A cleaner online menu, and ranked matches that flow into the next one.** Your rank sits up front, finding a match is one big button, and when a ranked match ends you go straight back in the queue or to the menu instead of an empty room.

> ✅ No forced update: 0.6.0 still plays online with 0.6.1.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🧭 The online menu, rebuilt

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gtko/ai-slop-arena/main/docs/releases/img/v0.6.1-lobby.png" alt="The new online menu: you, find a match, friends" width="100%"></p>

- **You**: your brawler, your name and your rank emblem (🥉 Bronze to 👑 Legend) with a bar showing the RP left to the next tier.
- **Find a match**: ranked and cross-platform, one big button, with the platforms you can meet.
- **Friends**: create a room or join one with a code, as clear tiles.
- On phones held sideways the three blocks sit side by side on one screen.

### 🔁 After a ranked match
- The result screen shows the RP won or lost, then **Play again** (straight back in the queue) or **Menu**.
- No more landing in a private room nobody else is in, and the address bar no longer shows a room link for matchmade rooms (they are not for sharing).

### 🔒 Privacy policy
- A privacy policy page (English and French) for the app stores: [/privacy.html](https://ai-slop-arena.gtux-prog.workers.dev/privacy.html).
